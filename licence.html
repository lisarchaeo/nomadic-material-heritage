Reusing this material

The photographs, films and interviews come from the Endangered Material Knowledge Programme collection at the British Museum and are published under a Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 licence. In short, you may copy and adapt the material if you credit the photographer and the programme, do not use it commercially, and share anything you make from it under the same licence.

Each item on this site names its photographer and links to its record in the repository, where the full-resolution original and the formal citation can be found.

Corrections

Names, places, dates and credits may be wrong or incomplete, and some records name a rights owner who is not the photographer. Corrections are welcome and are applied to this site as they arrive.

Removal requests
