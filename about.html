About

Welcome! This website displays material from the British Museum’s Endangered Material Knowledge Programme (EMKP)-funded project “Nomadic Material Heritage: Documenting Textile and Animal Hide Crafts in Western Mongolia”, directed by Dr. Kristen Pearson.

This material is under a Creative Commons BY-NC-SA 4.0 license. Each item credits its photographer and links to the British Museum repository.

You can find out more about the project here: the project in the British Museum repository.

This website has been built and maintained by Lisa Randisi.
